# Emerald reviewed progress variables

Generated by `scripts/extract-pokemon-progress-variables.mjs` from the reviewed candidate catalog and the pinned PRET checkout. Do not edit this report by hand.

18 raw 16-bit variables are retained under `SaveData.progressVariables` and exposed by `get_game_state(section="progressVariables")`. Missing values are unavailable, not zero. Numeric zero is not automatically proof of a choice or quest completion. Ruby/Sapphire interpretations are not included in this extraction.

## Roaming legendary choice

Read VAR_ROAMER_POKEMON only after the TV choice has initialized the roamer.

- `VAR_ROAMER_POKEMON` (`0x40d5`, key `roamerPokemon`): Raw roaming species choice: 0 = Latias, 1 = Latios. Zero is also the initial value; this is not proof of a choice before the postgame TV scene finishes.
- `VAR_LITTLEROOT_HOUSES_STATE_MAY` (`0x4082`, key `littlerootHouseMayState`): Raw player-house scene state. The TV-choice script sets this to 4 after InitRoamer; combine with the roaming flag before interpreting the species choice.
- `VAR_LITTLEROOT_HOUSES_STATE_BRENDAN` (`0x408c`, key `littlerootHouseBrendanState`): Raw player-house scene state. The TV-choice script sets this to 4 after InitRoamer; this value alone is not a capture record.

## Fossil revival status

Combine VAR_FOSSIL_RESURRECTION_STATE and VAR_WHICH_FOSSIL_REVIVED with the profile-specific received-fossil flag.

- `VAR_FOSSIL_RESURRECTION_STATE` (`0x40c4`, key `fossilResurrectionState`): Raw Devon revival phase: 1 = submitted and regenerating; 2 = ready to receive. State 0 is initial or reset after collection, so combine with received-fossil flag and fossil species value.
- `VAR_WHICH_FOSSIL_REVIVED` (`0x40c5`, key `whichFossilRevived`): Raw submitted fossil: 1 = Root Fossil/Lileep, 2 = Claw Fossil/Anorith. It can remain after collection and must be combined with the revival phase.

## New Mauville generator sidequest

Generator shutdown and collecting the TM are separate saved facts. Do not treat the state of the generator as proof the reward was collected.

- `VAR_NEW_MAUVILLE_STATE` (`0x40ba`, key `newMauvilleState`): Raw generator state: 2 means the stop switch was activated. Combine with FLAG_GOT_BASEMENT_KEY_FROM_WATTSON and FLAG_GOT_TM_THUNDERBOLT_FROM_WATTSON to distinguish acceptance, generator shutdown, and reward collection.

## Trick House puzzle progression

Retain level, puzzle phases, entrance state, and pending prize independently. Initial zero and puzzle-specific states are not proof that a puzzle is currently accessible or that its prize was collected.

- `VAR_TRICK_HOUSE_LEVEL` (`0x4044`, key `trickHouseLevel`): Raw puzzle progression: entrance script routes values 0 through 7 to puzzles 1 through 8; 8 selects the finished-puzzles branch. Initial zero does not prove the quest has started.
- `VAR_TRICK_HOUSE_FOUND_TRICK_MASTER` (`0x40a6`, key `trickHouseFoundMaster`): Raw current-puzzle hide-and-seek flag stored as a variable. Reset for new puzzles; not permanent whole-quest completion.
- `VAR_TRICK_HOUSE_ENTRANCE_STATE` (`0x40a7`, key `trickHouseEntranceState`): Raw entrance dialogue/scene state. Interpret using the entrance script, not as a count of completed puzzles.
- `VAR_TRICK_HOUSE_PRIZE_PICKUP` (`0x40c1`, key `trickHousePrizePickup`): Raw pending-reward state: the end-room script sets 1 when the bag or decoration storage is full and the prize cannot be delivered. Reward branches clear it to 0; zero is also initial, so combine with level and entrance state.
- `VAR_TRICK_HOUSE_PUZZLE_1_STATE` (`0x40ab`, key `trickHousePuzzle1State`): Raw puzzle 1 script state. It is a per-puzzle phase, not a boolean or a count; consult that puzzle script and the entrance/end scripts.
- `VAR_TRICK_HOUSE_PUZZLE_2_STATE` (`0x40ac`, key `trickHousePuzzle2State`): Raw puzzle 2 script state. It is a per-puzzle phase, not a boolean or a count; consult that puzzle script and the entrance/end scripts.
